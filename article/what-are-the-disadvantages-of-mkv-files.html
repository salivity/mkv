What Are the Disadvantages of MKV Files?

While the MKV (Matroska) container format is highly praised for its ability to hold an unlimited number of video, audio, and subtitle tracks in a single file, it does come with several notable drawbacks. This article provides a straightforward look at the primary disadvantages of using MKV files, including hardware and software compatibility issues, larger file sizes, challenges in video editing, and higher resource demands.

Limited Device and Software Compatibility

The most significant disadvantage of MKV is its lack of universal compatibility. Unlike the widely supported MP4 format, many devices and platforms do not natively support MKV playback.

  • Mobile Devices: Apple's iOS devices (iPhones and iPads) do not support MKV natively, requiring users to download third-party media players.
  • Smart TVs and Game Consoles: Older smart TVs, legacy Blu-ray players, and older gaming consoles often fail to recognize or play MKV files.
  • Web Browsers: Most major web browsers cannot stream MKV files directly, making them unsuitable for web-based video distribution.

Larger File Sizes

Because MKV is a container designed to preserve maximum quality, it often contains multiple high-definition audio tracks, multiple subtitle formats, and high-bitrate video streams. While this is great for preserving media quality, it results in significantly larger file sizes compared to more highly compressed formats like MP4 or WebM. This makes MKV files less ideal for streaming, uploading, or storing on devices with limited storage space.

Poor Video Editing Support

If you plan to edit video footage, MKV is generally a poor choice. Industry-standard video editing software—such as Adobe Premiere Pro, Apple Final Cut Pro, and DaVinci Resolve—either offers limited support for MKV or does not support it at all. Editors must often convert MKV files to edit-friendly formats like MP4, MOV, or ProRes before they can begin editing, adding an extra, time-consuming step to the workflow.

Higher Resource Consumption

Due to the complexity of the MKV container and the high-quality codecs it often houses, decoding these files requires more processing power. Playing high-bitrate MKV files on older hardware can lead to lag, stuttering, and audio-video desynchronization. Additionally, the extra CPU and GPU processing required can drain the battery life of laptops and mobile devices much faster than playing more optimized formats.
