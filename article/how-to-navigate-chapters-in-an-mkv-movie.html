How to Navigate Chapters in an MKV Movie

Navigating between chapters in an MKV (Matroska) movie is a simple process that depends on the media player you use. Because MKV files support embedded chapter markers, most modern video players allow you to skip directly to specific scenes using keyboard shortcuts, on-screen controls, or navigation menus. This guide outlines the quickest ways to jump between chapters in the most popular media players on Windows, Mac, and mobile devices.

VLC Media Player (Windows, Mac, Linux)

VLC is the most common player for MKV files and offers multiple ways to navigate chapters:

  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press the N key on your keyboard to skip to the next chapter. Press the P key to return to the previous chapter.
  • Top Menu Bar: Click on Playback in the top menu, hover over Chapter, and select the specific chapter you want to watch.
  • Right-Click Menu: Right-click anywhere on the video screen during playback, hover over Playback, select Chapter, and click your desired chapter.

MPC-HC (Media Player Classic)

MPC-HC is a lightweight, Windows-only player favored for playing high-quality MKV files:

  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press the Page Down key to skip to the next chapter. Press the Page Up key to go back to the previous chapter.
  • On-Screen Navigation: Click the Skip Forward or Skip Backward buttons on the control bar (the buttons with a vertical line next to the arrows).
  • Seek Bar Visuals: MPC-HC displays small tick marks on the playback timeline representing chapter starts. Hovering over these ticks will display the chapter name or number.

PotPlayer (Windows)

PotPlayer is a highly customizable media player with robust chapter support:

  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press Page Down to move to the next chapter and Page Up to move to the previous chapter.
  • Right-Click Menu: Right-click the video window, go to Playback, hover over Chapter, and select your destination from the sub-menu.

IINA (macOS)

For Mac users, IINA is a modern media player designed specifically for macOS that handles MKV files seamlessly:

  • On-Screen Controls: Click the Chapter list icon (which looks like a bulleted list) on the control bar to open a sidebar displaying all available chapters with timestamps.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Use the Shift + Right Arrow key to jump to the next chapter, and Shift + Left Arrow to go back to the previous one.
  • Menu Bar: Click Playback in the macOS menu bar, select Chapters, and choose your chapter.

Mobile Devices (Android and iOS)

If you are watching MKV movies on a smartphone or tablet using apps like MX Player or VLC for Mobile:

  • VLC Mobile: Tap the screen to reveal the controls, tap the More options (three dots) icon, select Chapters, and choose your chapter.
  • MX Player: Tap the screen, tap the Menu (three dots) in the top right, select Play, then Chapter, and make your selection.
