Can MKV Encapsulate RealVideo and RealAudio?

The Matroska (MKV) multimedia container format fully supports the encapsulation of legacy RealVideo and RealAudio streams. Because Matroska was architected to be an open, extensible, and codec-agnostic multimedia wrapper, it includes native specifications for carrying proprietary RealMedia formats without requiring re-encoding. This article explains how MKV handles legacy RealMedia streams, the specific codecs supported, the multiplexing process, and playback considerations.

Matroska Specifications for RealMedia

The Matroska specification explicitly defines codec IDs designed to store RealMedia data natively. Instead of stripping or transcoding the original compressed bitstream, MKV wraps the original frames alongside the initialization headers required by the decoder.

The standard Matroska codec identifiers for RealMedia include:

  • RealVideo: V_REAL/RV10, V_REAL/RV20, V_REAL/RV30, and V_REAL/RV40 (frequently used in RMVB files).
  • RealAudio: A_REAL/14_4 (early voice codec), A_REAL/28_8, A_REAL/COOK (Cooker), A_REAL/SIPR (Sipro), A_REAL/RALF (RealAudio Lossless Format), and A_REAL/ATRC (Sony ATRAC3).

Multiplexing Legacy Streams into MKV

Remuxing RealMedia content (typically found in .rm, .ra, or .rmvb files) into an .mkv container is a lossless process. Software such as MKVToolNix (mkvmerge) can read RealMedia containers directly, extract the underlying audio and video tracks, and encapsulate them into an MKV file without altering the compression quality.

During this process, the multiplexer extracts the necessary initialization private data from the RealMedia file and places it into the CodecPrivate element of the Matroska track headers, ensuring compliant players can initialize the decoders correctly.

Advantages of Encapsulation

Wrapping legacy streams in MKV provides several operational benefits:

  • Modern Feature Integration: Legacy streams can be merged with modern subtitle formats (such as SRT or ASS), chapter markers, and additional audio tracks.
  • Fixing Container Limitations: The legacy RealMedia container has limited indexing and metadata capabilities; moving to MKV improves seeking accuracy and streaming performance.
  • Preserved Quality: Because the audio and video are not re-encoded, the conversion process is fast and avoids generation loss.

Playback Compatibility

While the MKV container successfully houses RealVideo and RealAudio tracks, successful playback still requires a media player capable of decoding these legacy formats. Modern software players utilizing the FFmpeg backend—such as VLC Media Player, MPV, and MPC-HC—can natively decode RealMedia streams inside MKV containers without requiring original RealNetworks software or proprietary DirectShow filters.
