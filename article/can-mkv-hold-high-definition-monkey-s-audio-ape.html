Can MKV Hold High-Definition Monkey's Audio APE?

The Matroska (MKV) container fully supports high-definition audio encoded in Monkey's Audio (APE) format. Thanks to the open and highly flexible architecture of the Matroska specification, users can mux standard and high-resolution APE audio tracks into MKV video files or standalone MKA audio files. This article explains how Matroska handles the APE codec, how to mux high-definition APE streams into MKV containers, and what to consider regarding playback compatibility.

Technical Compatibility of MKV and APE

Matroska was designed to be codec-agnostic, meaning it can encapsulate almost any modern video, audio, or subtitle stream without altering the underlying data. Monkey's Audio is a popular lossless audio compression format that supports high-resolution specifications, including 24-bit depth and sample rates up to 192 kHz.

When packaged inside an MKV or MKA container:

  • Bit-Perfect Preservation: The APE audio stream remains intact as a bit-for-bit lossless copy of the original source.
  • Metadata and Chapters: Matroska allows rich metadata, track tags, and chapter markers to accompany the high-resolution APE track.
  • Multichannel Audio: High-definition multichannel configurations supported by Monkey's Audio can also be stored within the container.

How to Mux High-Definition APE into MKV

Muxing Monkey's Audio into a Matroska container requires standard multimedia tools such as MKVToolNix or FFmpeg.

Using MKVToolNix (GUI / mkvmerge)

  1. Open MKVToolNix GUI.
  2. Add your video source and the .ape audio file to the source files list.
  3. Verify the track properties (language, default track flag, and track name).
  4. Click Start multiplexing to output an .mkv (or .mka if audio-only) file.

Using FFmpeg

You can combine a video stream and an APE audio stream without re-encoding by running:

ffmpeg -i video.mp4 -i audio.ape -c copy output.mkv

The -c copy flag ensures the APE stream is multiplexed directly without any loss in audio quality.

Playback and Decoder Support

While the MKV container successfully stores high-definition Monkey's Audio streams, playback depends on the media player's ability to decode the APE format from inside a Matroska container.

  • Software Players: Modern desktop players like VLC Media Player, MPV, and MPC-HC (with LAV Filters) can demux and decode high-resolution APE tracks from MKV containers natively.
  • Hardware and Smart TVs: Standalone hardware media players, smart TVs, and streaming devices typically support standard MKV codecs like AAC, AC3, DTS, or FLAC. Most hardware devices do not contain a hardware or software decoder for Monkey's Audio. For maximum hardware compatibility, converting APE to FLAC before muxing into MKV is recommended.
